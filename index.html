You've known me for the last 8 years as The Jessica Pinili Podcast.

But somewhere between building businesses, boarding planes, changing my mind, falling in love, turning 33 and collecting approximately 47 personality upgrades… I outgrew her.

So, welcome to the new era.

Voice Message, Sent. is basically the voice note I'd send you at 11pm because I need to tell you something before I forget. Business, mindset, relationships, travel, wellness, identity, money, life transitions and whatever else is currently living rent-free in my brain.

Think: the group chat where someone finally says the thing everyone else was thinking.

I'm 33 now. I've been online for years. I've built things, burnt things down, rebuilt them better, changed cities, changed seasons and changed my mind more times than I can count.
